A quiz a day keeps learning sharp.

AQADAY (solo subscription) allows you to take charge of your own learning by self assigning daily quizzes of selected topics for your daily practice.

Available for the following subjects (aligned to Singapore MOE syllabus):

  1. Secondary 1 Mathematics (G1-G3)

  2. Secondary 1 Science (G1-G3)

  3. Secondary 2 Mathematics (G1-3)

  4. Secondary 2 Science (G1-G3)

How it works?

Parents

Set up your organisation, manage your classes, maintain your organisation’s question repository.

Students

Answer a quiz a day, build your streak, climb the class leaderboard.

Students

Learn a little daily, watch it add up.

Have full ownership of your learning by selecting questions from our curated question banks* and assign them to yourself as daily quizzes. Assign daily quizzes based on your own revision and learning plan.

*question banks are aligned to Singapore MOE syllabus requirements and learning objectives
