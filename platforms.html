IMPACTGROUND

(Online Creation Platform)

IMPACTGROUND is a webapp that uses vibe-coding (using AI to code) to inspire creativity in youths and families. Participants will use vibe-coding to co-create an online world together.

AQADAY - A Quiz A Day

(Organisation Subscription)

AQADAY is a webapp that brings daily quizzes to your school or educational organisation. Admins manage teachers and question banks. Teachers track student progress. Students earn points and climb the leaderboard. Consistency, made simple.
