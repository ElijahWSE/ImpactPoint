Saturday, February 28, 2026

10:00 AM – 12:00 PM

Knowledge Park 443 Pasir Ris Drive 6, #02-04 Singapore, 510443 Singapore (map)

Google Calendar · ICS

In this session, participants will be guided to create a simple webapp of their choice.

Through the process of creating their own webapp, participants will learn to:

  1. Create basic prompts for AI model to generate a code for a webapp

  2. Refine prompts to generate improved versions of their webapp

  3. Apply design thinking guidelines to improve the usability of their webapp

No coding experience needed!

Each participant will be required to bring a wifi enabled laptop.

Fees:

  • $50 per family (1 student + 1 or 2 parents)

  • $35 per student (individual, Primary 4 and above)
