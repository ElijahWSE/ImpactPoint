A quiz a day keeps learning sharp.

AQADAY helps schools and educational organisations run daily quizzes that build knowledge, streaks, and healthy competition amongst students.

How it works?

Organisations

Set up your organisation, manage your classes, maintain your organisation’s question repository.

Teachers

Schedule questions for your class, track student submissions and progress.

Students

Answer a quiz a day, build your streak, climb the class leaderboard.

Organisations

One platform for your whole organisation.

Organisations can maintain a private question repository, organise questions into subjects and topics, allowing teachers to push quizzes to their classes with ease.

Teachers

Schedule quizzes in minutes.

As a teacher, you get to schedule daily quizzes for your students with ease - import quizzes day by day, or mass import quizzes in just a few clicks.

Students

Learn a little daily, watch it add up.

Student answers one quiz everyday. Earn points for participation, correct answers, and bonus points for speed and streak. Rise up the ranks and compete with classmates to be the top of the leaderboard.

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes you different from other learning platforms?

Rather than using preset question banks that may not align to what will be useful for the students, we offer organisations the flexibility in creating and managing their own question banks that aligns to their learning objectives for their students.

Getting started is simple. Reach out through our contact form or schedule a call—we’ll walk you through the next steps and answer any questions along the way.
